# Benchmark authoritative reviews
Select 3-8 directly competing reviews, prioritizing editorial credibility, topical overlap, and recent comprehensive work. Record bibliographic identity and actual accessible text. For each: thesis, scope boundaries, organizing axis, comparison tables, evidence assessment, limitations, and contribution. Extract reusable rhetorical moves, not copyrighted paragraphs. Build a differentiation matrix: what the existing reviews cover, what they omit, and which new synthesis the manuscript can support. Do not equate impact factor with review quality. Mark any missing full text explicitly.
