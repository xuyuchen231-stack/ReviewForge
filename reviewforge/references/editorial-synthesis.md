# Evidence-led editorial synthesis

Use when a review reads like a textbook, a catalogue, or repeated short definitions.

Before drafting, identify the question answered by the subsection and what the reader should be able to judge afterwards. Group studies by a meaningful comparison axis, not by citation key, download order, or author name. A historical progression is useful only when explaining which assumption or capability changed.

Reverse-outline the draft silently: each paragraph contributes a claim, its evidence and an interpretation that advances the section answer. Expand the missing reasoning between findings; do not expand synonyms, general background or repeated caveats. These are functions, not a compulsory repeated paragraph pattern.

Compare tasks, measurements, conditioning inputs, validation split, baseline tuning, scale and outcome where the supplied evidence supports them. Preserve supported quantities. Different outcomes, datasets or splits may make a numerical ranking meaningless; explain that difference rather than inventing a common leaderboard. Distinguish observed mechanisms, plausible explanations and proposed tests.

Retain relevant contrary findings. Clear writing must not hide unfavorable evidence, move goalposts or remove material limitations. End with a conditional judgment about when an approach is justified and a specific unresolved question or discriminating test where warranted. Sparse evidence warrants a narrower conclusion, not invented disagreement or filler.

Review all substantive child headings within the existing five criteria. Quote actual candidate passages and identify the concrete missing comparison or inference. Reuse the existing evidence cards and review call; do not add a separate stylistic model loop or install a collection of overlapping agents.

For numbered inline bibliographies, check first occurrence in the final PDF, including table/figure float movement and compressed numerical ranges. Repeated citations retain their assigned number. Merge explicit, unambiguous same-DOI aliases only in an exported copy and record the mapping; never guess identity from similar titles or comments. Keep the publisher's author-year ordering when applicable. Do not claim this helper handles arbitrary macros, external BibTeX styles or unexpanded multifile projects.

Respect the author's requested columns. A successful compiler exit does not justify switching an entire two-column manuscript to one column. Reflow overwide displays locally while retaining their mathematics, use valid spanning-float placement, and check the resulting pages. Preserve a source/PDF/auxiliary-numbering pair when a bounded rebuild fails.

Plan manuscript figures around distinct scientific questions. Use source-linked, explicitly conceptual diagrams when no comparable numerical dataset is available; do not invent measurements, convert unknowns to negative findings or redraw a table merely to inflate figure count. A project-specific publication_figure_plan.json can provide the checked sources, roles, captions and insertion headings for local rendering. The runtime's four-panel concept template is optional, not a universal paper structure. Package only the main document and referenced assets for Overleaf, never credentials or unrelated drafts.
