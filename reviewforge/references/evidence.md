# Evidence protocol
Every empirical assertion needs a stable claim ID, cited source ID, original excerpt, locator, and scope conditions. Verify source metadata separately. Exact excerpts must be verbatim from user-supplied authorized text; verify against originals. A metadata match does not establish that a claim is supported. A reviewer model may assess entailment, but do not count it as independent factual confirmation. No source, no excerpt, or uncertain interpretation means unresolved. Do not insert unresolved assertions into the accepted manuscript. Preserve all negative findings and conflicting evidence.
